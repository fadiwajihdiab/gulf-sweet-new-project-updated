## Recommended analytical method for the next phase
This section provides continuation guidance, not new approved business policy.
- Normalize prices and costs to a consistent VAT basis, with explicit treatment of recoverable input VAT.
- Gross profit/box = net selling price − full box COGS.
- Gross margin = gross profit ÷ net selling price.
- Markup = gross profit ÷ cost. Never confuse 30% markup with 30% margin.
- Price for a proposed gross margin m = cost ÷ (1 − m). Add any variable incentives/costs when assessing contribution, and label it separately from accounting gross margin.
- Treat discount scenarios, sell-out incentives, and expected returns as explicit scenario inputs.
- Do not use collections less receivables as a profit calculation.
- Validate scale proposals against historical volume, route capacity, actual reorder cycles, demand, mix, contribution, and collection behavior.
